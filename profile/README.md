## What it is

Mistress Chat is a browser-based AI companion platform. You pick a ready character or create one with a name, look and personality. Conversations can stay in one thread so earlier details are more likely to return. Images of the character can be generated on paid plans. Voice replies are also available on higher tiers.

It is built for people who want a character they can shape and come back to, not a one-off chat. The free start is usually enough to see the tone. Paid plans unlock more messages, pictures and voice.

## How people use it

Most start by opening a character and sending a few messages. If the reply style fits, they stay in that thread or build their own. The builder lets you set appearance, personality and a short background. After that the chat is the main part. Pictures and voice are extras that sit on the paid side.

Some people use it for short daily check-ins. Others keep longer scenes running over several days. Memory is better on paid plans. Free tiers forget sooner.

## What to check first

- How many free messages you get. A handful is not the same as a daily allowance.
- What the paid plan actually unlocks: more chat, images, voice, or just higher limits.
- Whether you can cancel from the account page.
- Do not put real names, phone numbers or other personal details in the chat.

A few days of free use is usually enough to know if the character holds the scene. After that the decision is about media and how long you want to stay.
